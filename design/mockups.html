Recommended stack

Goal: nearly free to run, nothing to patch, and a password-reset and login system you don't have to write yourself.

Front endNext.js web app

One responsive site for phone and desktop. Members can "Add to Home Screen" so it feels like an app.

Login, data, photosSupabase

Handles accounts, long-lived logins, reset-password emails, the Postgres database and photo storage. Board vs member rules enforced in the database.

HostingVercel

Deploys automatically from this GitHub repo. Free hobby tier covers this traffic.

EmailResend (or Amazon SES)

Sends board blasts and reply notifications from your own domain so they don't land in spam.

  • Persistent login: Supabase keeps a refresh token in the browser, so people stay signed in until they tap Log out. A "Log out everywhere" button covers a lost phone.
  • Password reset: built in. The user enters their email, gets a one-time link, and picks a new password.
  • Adding members: a board member enters a name, email and address. The person gets an invite email to set their password. No public sign-up.
  • Maintenance: no servers to patch. The main chores are renewing the domain yearly and keeping the free database active (Supabase pauses free projects after a week with no use, which a live neighborhood site won't hit).

Home feed

Everything on one page, newest first. A board notice can be pinned to the top.

For sale & free

Listings with a price or marked Free. They archive themselves after 30 days unless the seller renews.

Listing and replies

Shown as the seller sees it. Buyers' replies are private between them and the seller.

Event with RSVP and comments

Anyone can post an event. Questions and answers are public so everyone sees them.

Board announcement and email blast

Only board members see this. One form posts to the site, emails everyone, or both.

Member management

Board-only. Members are grouped by house so you can see which homes have nobody signed up.
